# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Pokémon fans who play synchronous party minigames with friends, from mobile or
desktop browsers. Registered players care about quick room entry, clear game
state and trustworthy results. Administrators operate the production service
and need private, read-only visibility into users, live rooms and historical
activity without exposing gameplay secrets or signalling observation to
players.

## Product Purpose

Pokémon Universe provides server-authoritative multiplayer minigames, persistent
player profiles and reliable reconnect behavior. It should make hosting and
playing sessions effortless while giving authorized operators enough evidence
to understand adoption, active usage and interrupted sessions safely.

## Brand Personality

Playful, competitive and trustworthy. Copy is direct and friendly in Spanish;
the interface feels energetic without compromising clarity during timed play or
dense operational review.

## Anti-references

- Generic enterprise dashboards detached from the established Pokémon Universe
  visual language.
- Decorative admin surfaces that obscure tables, filters or operational status.
- Player-facing surveillance cues or any exposure of private roles, answers,
  votes, game state or credentials.
- Dense desktop-only layouts that become unusable on a phone.

## Design Principles

- Keep decisions and private game state server-authoritative.
- Make the current room and game state understandable at a glance.
- Use progressive detail: lead with operational summaries, then searchable
  records and participant-level detail.
- Reuse one coherent interaction vocabulary across play, profile and admin
  surfaces.
- Prefer durable audit facts over inferred or silently discarded activity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic
landmarks and tables, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior and layouts
that work from 320px mobile widths through wide desktop screens. Never rely on
color alone to communicate status.
